## 🤔 Why TataTokBox?

You know the routine 😩 You want that classic TikTok text box (white box, bold black text, rounded corners), so you open TikTok, type it, screenshot it, crop it, and drag it into your editor. Or you rebuild it by hand in Premiere or CapCut, and it never looks quite right.

**TataTokBox skips all of that.** 🙌 Type your text, tap some emoji, download a transparent PNG that looks just like TikTok's, and drop it on your timeline. Done ✅

## 🎬 Who it's for

- 🎞️ **Video editors** adding TikTok-native captions to client videos
- 📱 **Content creators** posting the same clip to TikTok, Reels and Shorts
- 📣 **UGC and ad makers** who want overlays that feel native to the platform
- 💁 **Anyone** who doesn't want to open a video editor just to make one text box

## 💖 Features

- 🅰️ **Looks like TikTok:** TikTok Sans (TikTok's own "Classic" font) and the same rounded box with smooth curves between lines
- 😍 **iOS emoji keyboard:** real Apple emoji on every device, even Windows and Android, with Frequently Used, search, categories and skin tones 👋🏻👋🏽👋🏿
- 🎨 **TikTok's 28 colors** and all four styles: solid box, see-through box, no box, and outline
- 📏 **Size slider** and **left / center / right** alignment
- 🖼️ **Transparent PNG** at 4× resolution, cropped tight to the box, sharp on 1080p and 4K timelines
- 📋 **Copy to clipboard** to paste straight into your editor
- 🌄 **Preview on your own picture** to see how it looks over a frame of your video
- 📲 **Works on phones:** Download opens the share sheet so you can save to Photos

## 🪄 How to use it

1. ✍️ Type your text. Press Enter for a new line, or leave an empty line to split it into separate boxes.
2. 😂 Add emoji from the keyboard. Long-press (or right-click) for skin tones.
3. 🎨 Pick a color, a style (the **A** button) and an alignment, and set the size with the slider.
4. ⬇️ Hit **Download** and drop the PNG into CapCut, Premiere Pro, Final Cut, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects or anything else.

## 🛠️ Built with

- ⚛️ [React](https://react.dev) + [Vite](https://vite.dev) + TypeScript
- 🔤 [TikTok Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/TikTok+Sans) (SIL Open Font License)

- 📸 [modern-screenshot](https://github.com/qq15725/modern-screenshot) for PNG export
- 🌐 Hosted on GitHub Pages and deployed automatically on every push to `main`
